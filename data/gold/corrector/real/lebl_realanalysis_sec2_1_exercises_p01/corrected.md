Exercise 2.1.16: Let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence. Suppose there are two convergent subsequences $\{x_{n_i}\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ and $\{x_{m_i}\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$. Suppose

$$\lim_{i \to \infty} x_{n_i} = a \quad \text{and} \quad \lim_{i \to \infty} x_{m_i} = b,$$

where $a \neq b$. Prove that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is not convergent, without using Proposition 2.1.17.

Exercise 2.1.17 (Tricky): Find a sequence $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ such that for every $y \in \mathbb{R}$, there exists a subsequence $\{x_{n_i}\}_{i=1}^{\infty}$ converging to $y$.

Exercise 2.1.18 (Easy): Let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence and $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Suppose for every $\epsilon > 0$, there is an $M$ such that $|x_n - x| \leq \epsilon$ for all $n \geq M$. Show that $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = x$.

Exercise 2.1.19 (Easy): Let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence and $x \in \mathbb{R}$ such that there exists a $k \in \mathbb{N}$ such that for all $n \geq k$, $x_n = x$. Prove that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ converges to $x$.

Exercise 2.1.20: Let $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence and define a sequence $\{y_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ by $y_{2k} := x_{k^2}$ and $y_{2k-1} := x_k$ for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Prove that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ converges if and only if $\{y_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ converges. Furthermore, prove that if they converge, then $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \lim_{n \to \infty} y_n$.

Exercise 2.1.21: Show that the 3-tail of the sequence defined by $x_n := \frac{n}{n^2+16}$ is monotone decreasing. Hint: Suppose $n \geq m \geq 4$ and consider the numerator of the expression $x_n - x_m$.

Exercise 2.1.22: Suppose that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is a sequence such that the subsequences $\{x_{2n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$, $\{x_{2n-1}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$, and $\{x_{3n}\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ all converge. Show that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is convergent.

Exercise 2.1.23: Suppose that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is a monotone increasing sequence that has a convergent subsequence. Show that $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ is convergent. Note: So Proposition 2.1.17 is an "if and only if" for monotone sequences.
